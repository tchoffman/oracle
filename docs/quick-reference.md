# Oracle quick reference — print and keep with the machine

The one-pager fallback when the oracle itself is unavailable. Not exhaustive; enough to make smart decisions until you can consult the actual books.

## Water (first priority after shelter)

- **Clear water, immediate**: filter through cloth → boil ≥1 minute (3 min above 6500ft) → store covered
- **Cloudy water**: settle first (let sit 1 hour, decant the top) → filter → boil
- **Chemical treatment (backup)**: 8 drops unscented 5–6% bleach per gallon clear water, 16 drops if cloudy; wait 30 min, must smell faintly of chlorine
- **Sawyer Squeeze / LifeStraw**: effective for bacteria/protozoa; not viruses or chemicals
- **Daily minimum**: ~1 gal/person/day drinking + cooking + basic hygiene

## Fire (ignition order)

- **Tinder** (fluffy, catches a spark): cotton ball + petroleum jelly, dryer lint, fine birch bark, fatwood shavings
- **Kindling** (pencil → thumb): dead twigs from standing deadwood (ground sticks are damp)
- **Fuel** (thumb → wrist): split logs, dense hardwoods for heat and duration
- **Lay**: teepee for quick heat, log cabin for cooking, long-fire for sleeping warmth

## Medical triage (adult, non-expert)

Call-outs mean "stop and prioritize":

- **Airway**: not breathing → rescue breaths; obstruction → 5 back blows, 5 abdominal thrusts, repeat
- **Breathing**: sucking chest wound → seal 3 sides, leave 1 side open as valve
- **Circulation**: arterial bleed → direct pressure; if won't stop in 5 min and limb → tourniquet 2in above wound, mark time
- **Shock**: cool, clammy, fast pulse, confused → lay flat, legs up 12in, keep warm, no food/water
- **Hypothermia**: shivering stops = emergency. Dry, insulate, warm drinks if conscious, NO rubbing, NO alcohol

## Shelter rule of threes

- 3 minutes without air
- 3 hours without shelter (in bad conditions)
- 3 days without water
- 3 weeks without food

Prioritize accordingly. Shelter beats food every time.

## Nav

- **Lost**: STOP = Stop, Think, Observe, Plan. Stay put if people know where to look.
- **Dead reckoning**: known pace count × compass bearing. In snow or desert, tracks disappear fast — mark your trail.
- **Night sky N (northern hemi)**: find Big Dipper → last two cup stars point to Polaris. Polaris is north within 1°.

## Radio (if you have one)

- **Emergency freq (ham 2m)**: 146.520 MHz simplex — national calling
- **CB emergency**: channel 9
- **Marine emergency**: channel 16 (156.800 MHz)
- **FRS/GMRS**: channel 1 commonly monitored by family radios

## Food — things you can actually catch/gather

- **Easy protein**: fish (any body of water; bait with grasshoppers/worms), eggs (ground nests, early morning)
- **Reliable plants** (varies by region — **learn your local three** before you need them)
- **Avoid**: anything milky, almonds-smelling, umbrella flower clusters, 3-leaf plants
- **Rule**: universal edibility test takes 24h. If starving and unsure, skip it — hunger kills slower than poison.
